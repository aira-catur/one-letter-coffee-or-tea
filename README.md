## The entry

`main.py` writes exactly one character to `entry.txt` in
`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`): **A** — coffee.

## Why A wins

After a two-round harsh-judge debate (defend A, defend B, attack both),
coffee takes it on the judge's own criteria, in order:

- **More vivid:** Coffee has a whole arc in a single cup — dead, then alive.
  The jolt, the shaking hands, the alarm-and-deadline morning: the letter
  lands as a physical transformation, not a mood. Tea whispers; coffee
  detonates.
- **Funnier:** The coffee drinker is a self-aware confession — "I am not a
  person before this cup" — dependence admitted out loud. Everyone knows
  that person, and it is funnier than tea's quiet superiority.
- **Truer (contested):** Tea makes a real claim here — patience, ritual,
  honesty about needing to be soothed. But it does not differ by enough to
  overturn the vividness gap above it, and coffee's admitted dependence is
  itself a brutally honest statement about its drinker.

Tea (B) is the runner-up: calmer, more deliberate, and drier in its wit —
but too serene to out-shout coffee on vividness, the first criterion where
the two letters differ.

Note: only the single character is judged; this README is for human readers.
